About Genji

Genji Shimada is a cybernetic ninja with a sharp mind and deadly precision. After a near-fatal encounter with his brother, he was saved by Overwatch's medical team and enhanced with advanced cybernetic technology. Now, he walks the path of balance, mastering both technology and his ninja heritage.

Abilities

  • Shuriken: Throws three deadly throwing stars in quick succession.
  • Deflect: Reflects incoming projectiles back at enemies.
  • Swift Strike: Dashes forward, slicing enemies in his path.
  • Dragonblade (Ultimate): Unleashes a powerful melee attack with his katana.
